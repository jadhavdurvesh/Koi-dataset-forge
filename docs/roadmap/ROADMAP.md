# 🗺️ KOI Dataset Forge Roadmap

> **Every heart has a story worth understanding.**

This roadmap outlines the long-term development plan for KOI Dataset Forge, KOI Hearts, and the KOI Emotional Intelligence Language Model.

---

# 🌸 Vision

KOI aims to become a human-centered AI system capable of understanding emotional context, communication, and relationships.

Rather than memorizing conversations, KOI learns from structured emotional experiences.
